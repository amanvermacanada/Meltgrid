# Final Keyword-to-Page Mapping

The 139 keywords in `04-keyword-research.csv` (and the Top 30 in `04a-top30-keyword-opportunities.csv`) cluster into 9 real content needs. Each cluster maps to **one** page/section — no keyword here gets its own dedicated page, per the brief's own rule against doorway pages and near-duplicate content.

| # | Cluster | Rows / aggregate volume* | Primary target | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cost & decision-making (installation cost, running cost, worth-it, comparisons) | 50 rows / ~13,750/mo | `/cost/` (primary), `/resources/heated-driveway-running-cost/` (running-cost subset) | Largest real cluster. `heated driveway cost` + `cost for a heated driveway` alone = 5,000/mo each. Ontario/Toronto cost variants (500/mo each) fold into `/cost/`'s new Ontario/GTA section, not a separate page. |

## Explicitly not targeted

- **DIY retail-purchase terms** (HeatTrak, Amazon, Costco, Home Depot mat searches) — different buyer, different product, not this business.
- **WarmlyYours brand-name searches** — real partner, but weak SEO value to target directly; a one-line mention on `/technology/` and `/hydronic-snow-melting/` (for the electric-side attribution that already exists) is enough.
- **Uponor brand-name searches** — no confirmed partnership; do not build content around a specific hydronic manufacturer until one is commercially confirmed.
